# Play home and app shell

Scope: the app shell (rail, header, wordmark) and the Play home screen; the voice and camera bar. Mode: Operate. Desktop and laptop first.

Audience and job: a pod member opening the site on game night to take a seat at the pod's table, or to practice with a deck. Content: open tables, the pod deck shelf, ready-made decks in every format, singleplayer, draft and sealed.

Constraints: never crowd the game board; never fight card art; not cartoonish, not corporate. Keep every existing route and function.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a freshly cracked booster pack. Every way in (play the pod, practice, draft) is a wrapper in its own bright colour; the game board stays a calm dark table. Refuses the category default of a dark navy, gold-trimmed fantasy client.

OWN-WORLD: Deep plum ground (#1a1030 family), hot prawn-pink rail, wrapper colour fields in pink, teal, violet; sun yellow reserved for "your turn / active" only; cream ink. Crimped zigzag seam on wrapper tops. Two line weights only. Bricolage Grotesque display, Figtree UI, tabular numerals for counts and life.

STORY: The visitor sees the pod's table and their own decks first, understands where to sit in one glance, and is seated in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: 88px pink rail with the prawn mark and five destinations. Top: "Prawnstars MTG" wordmark and who you are. Then three wrapper tiles in a 1.4/1/1 row (Play the pod, Practice vs AI, Draft and sealed), each with its primary action. Below: your pod deck shelf, then open tables beside ready-made decks. Voice and cameras dock bottom-left of the content.

FORM: Booster Pack, candidate 5 of 7 grounded; seed:6bde1e5f6dfa0152. Signature move: the crimped seam on every wrapper, and the rule that one colour means one job.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
